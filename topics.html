Puzzle topics

K-PUZZLE.COM has two themes and 100 topics in each. Open the lobby, pick a theme, then one of these names. English clues ask for Hangul answers — group names, song titles, characters, and plot points you already know as a fan.

Every topic keeps its own question bank. Beginner draws 5 clues, Intermediate 10, and Advanced 20. Random Mix pulls from the whole theme onto one board. For the rules of the grid, see How to Play.
