About K-PUZZLE.COM

K-PUZZLE.COM is a free, fan-made Hangul crossword for people who love K-POP, K-Drama, and Korean films. The home screen is the puzzle lobby. This page is only here to explain what the site is, who it is for, and how we treat the culture we borrow from.

You do not need an account. Open the site, pick a theme, pick a topic you already care about, and fill a crossword with Hangul syllables. Clues are written in English so global fans can play even if they are still learning to read Korean.

What you play

There are two themes: K-POP and K-Drama & Movies. Each theme has 100 topics — groups such as TWICE or films such as Squid Game. Every topic keeps a bank of fan questions. When you start a puzzle, the site draws clues from that bank onto one crossword.

Difficulty changes how many clues land on the board:

  • Beginner — 5 clues, shorter Hangul, gentler questions
  • Intermediate — 10 clues, more overlapping syllables
  • Advanced — 20 clues, a denser grid and longer answers

Play the same topic again and you can get a new draw. Random Mix pulls clues from every subject in that theme onto one board.

Why Hangul, not English answers

The grid is filled with Hangul syllables, not Roman letters. That is the point of the site. Fans already know the names, titles, and story beats; the crossword asks them to place the Korean spelling. A letter bank sits beside the board, so you tap tiles instead of typing on a Korean keyboard.

We built it this way because a lot of K-culture quizzes stay in English. K-PUZZLE.COM is a small practice space: you recall a comeback title or a character name, then lock in the syllables. It is a puzzle first, not a language course, but many players use it to get more comfortable reading Hangul they already hear in songs and subtitles.

A fan-made, unofficial site

K-PUZZLE.COM is an unofficial, independent project. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any entertainment agency, broadcaster, streaming service, or artist. Names of groups, songs, dramas, films, and characters belong to their respective rights holders. We use them only as familiar topics for fan questions.

If a rights holder wants a topic changed or removed, use Contact Us in the footer. We take those requests seriously.

Ads, privacy, and contact

The puzzles are free. The site may show advertisements, including ads served by Google AdSense, to help cover hosting and keep the project online. Third-party vendors may use cookies to serve and measure ads. You can read the details, including opt-out links, in our Privacy Policy.

Clear times stay on your device in local storage so this browser can show a rank after you finish. We do not require a login and we do not sell personal information. Questions, bug reports, and topic ideas can be sent through Contact Us. Include a reply email so we can write back.

If you want the rules of the board itself, see How to Play. Short answers to common questions live on the FAQ.
